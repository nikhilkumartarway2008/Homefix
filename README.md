# 🏠 HomeFix

> A modern on-demand home services marketplace that connects customers with trusted local professionals.

HomeFix is a full-stack home services platform where customers can discover and book professionals for household services such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, AC technicians, appliance repair, cleaning, and pest control.

The platform consists of three major applications:

- 📱 Customer App
- 🔧 Professional/Helper App
- 🖥️ Admin Dashboard

---

## ✨ Features

### 👤 Customer

- Phone number authentication
- OTP verification
- Location management
- Browse service categories
- Search for services
- View service details
- Describe household problems
- Upload photos
- Select date and time
- Add/manage addresses
- Find available professionals
- View professional profiles
- Book appointments
- Track booking status
- Call/chat with professionals
- Online/cash payment
- Booking history
- Notifications
- Ratings & reviews
- Customer support
- Emergency service booking

---
